## Events
No cast, windup, CP or action slot for passives. Resolve parent skill fully -> authoritative event -> eligibility -> collect passive candidates -> dedupe -> deterministic sort by passive ID/actor UID -> apply capped effect -> emit visual-only receipt. Child passive, combo, summon, reflection or DOT events do not trigger another passive (explicit before_combo_payload is pure modifier, not proc); no proc from failed cast/immune/overheal. All event buffs have ICD and duration; cooldown per owner instance+passive across targets; threshold downward crossing rearm only when above 40%, no proc from max HP/CP resize, death or self HP cost. For P3 CP threshold paid cost or hostile drain allowed. No trigger on death except actual valid kill by living owner; defeat source removes aura and temporary passive effects. Intrinsic P1 and continuous aura not dispellable; temporary event buff/debuff is dispellable by allowed effect, not bypass immunity. Counterplay deny event, leave aura radius, cleanse, defeat source, or block legal form. No auto ultimate, no additional attack/damage callback. Existing combat rules, source and adapter holds prevail.
