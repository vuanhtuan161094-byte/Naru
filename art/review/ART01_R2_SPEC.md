# ART-01 R2 — art direction & approval gate
Status: design only; D2 PNGs are retained as rejected-for-production low-detail reference, not approved sprites. Goal: modern HD pixel chibi, visible hard pixel edges and rich deliberate clusters, stylized anime proportions, readable silhouettes at runtime. Do not copy the advertisement, commercial sprite sheet, logos, or UI. User benchmark establishes visual genre, not pixel dimensions. Use original artwork; record source/rights of all assets. Naruto fan project is not licensed for public distribution.
## Candidate specifications
- Character cell 80x96 or 96x112, silhouette height ~70–85px; head 38–45% of visible height. Pixel grid aligned, no antialiased outline, 3–5 shading steps, light upper-left, consistent 1–2px outline. Palette per character 24–40 colors; not a mandatory hard limit. Compare 1x and 2x nearest-neighbor. Four sample archetypes: warm orange agile fighter, dark-haired swordsman, silver-haired masked veteran, dark-cloaked illusionist. Distinct silhouettes, not recolors of one template.
- Four diagonal directions NE/NW/SE/SW (mirror only for truly symmetric clothing; weapon hand, eyes, scars, insignia must be checked). Reference anchor at foot center, each animation stable. No rig or interpolation-generated limb movement. Idle 6 frames, walk 8, normal attack 8, cast 10, hit 4, fall 6; frame durations in ms authored per frame; these are proposals. 42 frames per direction, 168 per ninja, 672 for four samples. Battle 12 actors, movement on ground plane, y-sort from feet.
- VFX separate transparent layers: startup charge, travel, impact, residual; pixel-first, additive glow and particles secondary; core silhouette remains readable. Distinct chakra orb (blue), lightning arc (blue-white), protective spectral aura (warm red-orange), shadow control (violet). Environmental test: original forest arena and village panorama, clear traversable plane and fixed clickable buildings.
- UI: 1280x720 logical canvas proposal, landscape responsive safe areas, compact portraits and chakra/CD, skill list expanded for unlocked skills; optional cut-ins, no equipment sprite swaps. Avoid covering battle with particles or cut-ins.
## Review gates
G1: side-by-side four static designs compared at 1x/2x, owner chooses exact visual direction; D2 assets fail quality acceptance.
G2: four directions / real frame-by-frame animation; no alpha fringes, stable anchors, consistent equipment identity, no repeated static frames presented as full animation.
G3: three VFX on 12-actor static/animated test; visible charge, impact and fade; no full-screen glow.
G4: Godot integration, performance, screenshot review only after owner approval and start of implementation.
